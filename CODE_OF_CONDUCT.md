# Community Code of Conduct

NuvyraHub is committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on ideas, evidence, and technical outcomes rather than personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information without consent.
- Collaborate across experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and abusive or sexualized conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to `SECURITY_CONTACT_OR_COMMUNITY_EMAIL`. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Replace this placeholder with a monitored official contact before publishing.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and confidentially to the extent possible. They may edit or remove content, issue warnings, or restrict participation when necessary.
